Sprite assets for the Hack the North 2026 badge (320x240 screen, 42x42 icon).

gen1-16/        151 PNG, uniform 16x16 RGBA, National Dex order
                Cut from a community pixel-art sheet of all 151 Gen 1
                Pokemon by @balavicknesh. The sheet draws each creature on a
                16x16 canvas upscaled 6x; tools/cut_sheet.py recovers the
                native pixels, so these are the artist's exact art, not a
                downsample. One sprite (089 Muk) measured 17 wide and lost a
                single column of WebP bleed.

iconic-36/      Curated picks grouped by type, contributed separately.

app-icon-42.png 42x42, the launcher icon. Feed it to the IDE's Choose image,
                which converts to icon.bin (5,304 bytes, LVGL RGB565A8).

These are fan art of Nintendo / Game Freak characters. Treat them as the
hackathon assets they are; do not redistribute.

Regenerate
  python3 tools/cut_sheet.py --sheet     re-cut gen1-16 from the source sheet
  python3 tools/gen_dex16.py             rebuild app/dex16.txt from gen1-16
